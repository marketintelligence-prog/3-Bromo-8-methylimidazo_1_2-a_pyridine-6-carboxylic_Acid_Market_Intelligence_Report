Product Overview & Identity

Introduction

3-Bromo-8-methylimidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-6-carboxylic acid is a fused bicyclic N-heteroaromatic acid. It is built on the imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine core, which reviews describe as a "privileged scaffold" found in marketed drugs such as zolpidem, alpidem, olprinone, minodronic acid, zolimidine and soraprazan [Molecules 2025] [PMC review]. It has two orthogonal handles. The C3-bromide is used for Suzuki, Sonogashira and Buchwald couplings; 3-bromo-imidazo[1,2-a]pyridines are routinely converted into π-extended products by Suzuki–Miyaura coupling [RSC Adv 2018]. The C6-carboxylic acid is used for amide formation. The C8-methyl blocks a metabolic soft spot and tunes conformation. The same 6-carboxamide pattern appears in the potassium-competitive acid blockers (P-CABs) zastaprazan (approved in Korea, 2024) and linaprazan, but those carry an 8-benzylamino group, not 8-methyl [Zastaprazan].

Identity caution: Several close isomers are sold under similar names. Do not confuse with 6-bromo-8-methylimidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-2-carboxylic acid (CAS 866135-83-3), 8-bromo-6-methylimidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-3-carboxylic acid, the des-methyl 3-bromoimidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-6-carboxylic acid (CAS 886362-00-1), or 3-bromo-8-methyl-imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-2-carboxylic acid. Confirm the CAS number plus 1H NMR (C5-H and C7-H singlets, C2-H singlet, CH3 singlet) on every PO [ChemicalBook] [CymitQuimica] [Sigma/Leyan].

Industry Applications, End-Products & Application Market Sizes

Indirect role: This compound is never a finished product. It is an intermediate inside discovery and development programmes. All market sizes below are for the parent segment, not for this molecule.

A. Pharmaceutical Intermediates — Medicinal Chemistry Building Block (primary)

Its main use is as a bifunctional scaffold for 3-aryl/heteroaryl-8-methylimidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-6-carboxamides. The usual sequence is amide coupling at C6, then cross-coupling at C3, or the reverse. Imidazo[1,2-a]pyridine-6-carboxamides appear in IRAK4 inhibitor patents (US 2024/0116922), and 3-aryl imidazo[1,2-a]pyridines in kinase programmes (VEGFR2, FLT3, PI3Kα) [US20240116922] [EP1904494] [FLT3] [PI3Kα].
